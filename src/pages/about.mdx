It's not easy to put down tens of thousands of words in a narrative that all somehow have to make sense together in a satisfying way. I have the utmost admiration for what you have accomplished. Storytelling is a profound and transformative act.

      My intent as an editor is to help you refine your story to its fullest potential. I never want to take over your authorial voice.
    
      Your writing might already be strong, but everyone has blind spots, especially when it comes to a project they've been focused on for a long time. I offer fresh eyes with training in linguistics, editing, and storycraft. I'll ride along and point out potholes and missed turns, but I'll always remember that it's your vehicle, you're the driver, and it's your journey. It's _your_ story to tell. But I can help you make it stronger.
